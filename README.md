# Online Retail Sales Analysis

An exploratory data analysis project focused on uncovering sales trends, customer purchasing behavior, revenue patterns, and geographical demand in online retail transactions.

## Project Overview

This project explores online retail sales data to understand business performance, identify purchasing patterns, and generate meaningful insights through data analysis and visualization.

The analysis focuses on revenue trends over time, country-wise sales performance, customer contribution, and geographical distribution of retail demand.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and revenue performance.
- Identify seasonal trends and changes in revenue over time.
- Determine the top-performing countries based on sales revenue.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior and identify high-value customers.
- Explore geographical patterns in retail demand.
- Translate transactional data into meaningful business insights.

## Key Areas of Analysis

### 1. Annual Revenue Trends
- Analyze revenue performance over time.
- Identify seasonal patterns and fluctuations.
- Explore potential revenue growth trends and forecasting opportunities.

### 2. Top 10 Countries by Revenue
- Identify the highest-revenue-generating countries.
- Compare country-wise product revenue contributions.
- Understand geographical differences in sales performance.

### 3. Country Revenue Distribution
- Compare revenue distributions across countries using boxplots.
- Explore variations in sales performance.
- Identify differences between high-performing and lower-performing markets.

### 4. Top 10 Customers
- Rank customers based on their revenue contribution.
- Identify high-value customers.
- Understand customer concentration and purchasing contributions.

### 5. Geographical Demand Analysis
- Visualize sales distribution across geographical locations.
- Identify markets with stronger retail demand.
- Explore potential opportunities for market expansion.
